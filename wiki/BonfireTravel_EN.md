# Bonfire Fast Travel

**Bonfire Fast Travel** is a teleportation mechanic that allows the player to instantly travel between previously placed bonfires across the world.

---

## How it works
To initiate fast travel, the following conditions must be met:

1. **Be near a bonfire:** You must be standing in close proximity to any active bonfire in the world. If you are too far away, the game will notify you.
2. **Open the map:** Open the full-screen world map (default key `M`).
3. **Select a destination:** Locate the special bonfire icon on the map, hover over it, and **Left-Click** to teleport.

---

## Requirements and Cost

The fast travel system is balanced around the game's progression and comes with a cost:

* **Hardmode:** Teleportation between bonfires is only unlocked after the world enters **Hardmode**.
* **Humanity:** Each teleport consumes a certain amount of Humanity (this cost can be adjusted in the mod's configuration settings).
* **Post-Moon Lord:** After defeating the **Moon Lord**, bonfire teleportation becomes entirely **free** and no longer consumes Humanity.
